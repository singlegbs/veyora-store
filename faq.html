Frequently asked questions

How do I order?

Browse the shop, open a product to review its details, choose a quantity and add it to your cart. Open the cart and select Checkout. You can also contact Veyora on WhatsApp with the product name for help placing an order.

How do I pay?

The online checkout provides a Continue to Paystack step. The payment options available for your order are shown by Paystack during payment. Do not send card details by WhatsApp or email.

Do I need an account?

The checkout supports guest orders. Account sign-in is available separately.

How can I check delivery arrangements or product support?

Veyora delivers through Yango and SHAQ Express. Contact the store before ordering to confirm arrangements for your location and any product-specific return or warranty information. These details are not promised on this page.

How do I reach Veyora?

WhatsApp +233 53 818 1600 or hello@veyoragh.com.
